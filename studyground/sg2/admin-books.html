Forty chapters, one page

TOEFL Practice Book — B2 Level Up. Four books of ten chapters. Each chapter is authored once and becomes the student PDF, the answer key and teacher edition, a runnable exam set, the listening audio and the search index. This page shows what exists, what the checks caught, and the exact command that bakes the files.

Checks

Every gate is one of two levels. Stop means the chapter cannot become a working exam — build and publish stay locked until it is gone. Check means a human should look, but the chapter still saves. This is the same rule the set importer uses.

Chapter preview

The chapter as it will print — headings, instructions, questions, in page order. The correct choice is highlighted; turn off Answer key above to see the student edition.
